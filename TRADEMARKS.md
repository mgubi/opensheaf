# OpenSheaf: use of the name and the logo

The name **OpenSheaf** and the OpenSheaf logo and symbol (the dot mark, alone
or with the lettering, in `resources/brand/`) identify the specification
published at [opensheaf.org](https://opensheaf.org) and the work around it.
This policy says how they may be used, so that anyone reading the name knows
what it stands for. The specification itself is open (CC BY-SA 4.0), and
anyone may implement it, without asking; this policy is only about the name
and the logo.

The logo files are licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see
[`resources/brand/README.md`](resources/brand/README.md)); like every Creative
Commons licence, it grants no rights to the name or the logo as marks. Those
are governed by this policy.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Saying what something is.** Naming OpenSheaf to describe, truthfully,
  what a document, an archive, a tool or a service does with it: "an archive
  following OpenSheaf 0.1", "tools for OpenSheaf archives", "this collection
  is an OpenSheaf selection", "imports from OpenSheaf archives".
- **Pointing to OpenSheaf.** Using the logo or the symbol, unmodified, to
  link to [opensheaf.org](https://opensheaf.org) or to the specification, in
  a page, a talk, a paper or a poster.
- **"OpenSheaf-compatible".** Calling an archive or a collection
  "OpenSheaf-compatible" when it passes verification (section 14 of the
  specification), for instance with `osheaf verify`; calling a tool so when
  what it produces passes verification. Say which version of the
  specification (for instance "OpenSheaf 0.1").
- **Discussing OpenSheaf**, in any way, including criticism.

## Uses that need permission

- Suggesting that OpenSheaf, or its editor, endorses, certifies or
  maintains something it does not.
- Using "OpenSheaf" as, or in, the name of a product, a service, a project,
  an organisation or a domain name (for instance "OpenSheaf Cloud",
  "opensheaf-hosting.example"), except as a plain description of what the
  thing does with OpenSheaf, as above.
- Using a modified logo or symbol: other colours, shapes, proportions or
  lettering. Inverting black and white for a dark background, and scaling
  without changing the proportions, are not modifications.
- Using the name or the logo for a format or a specification that is not
  OpenSheaf (a fork may say "based on OpenSheaf", but not call itself
  OpenSheaf).

To ask for permission, or to report a misuse, open an
[issue](https://github.com/opensheaf/opensheaf.github.io/issues) or write to
the editor.

## Who holds the marks

The name and the logo are held by the editor of the specification,
[Massimiliano Gubinelli](https://mgubi.org/docs/main.html), for the
OpenSheaf project; they may be transferred to an organisation that takes
over the project. This policy may change; the version in this repository is
the one in force.
